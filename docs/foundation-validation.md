# Foundation validation ledger

Updated 23 September 2026. This ledger maps all 27 defects and 15 improvements to the implementation and regression checks.

The final integrated Playwright suite passed all 228 cases across Chromium, Firefox, and WebKit. It includes committed-popup navigation readiness, focused reading scenarios, and controlled playback coverage. A fresh pinned install, production build, route checks, and lint pass.

The local production UI matrix covered 288 combinations (Chromium, Firefox, and WebKit; 390, 768, and 1280 px; both themes; normal and reduced motion). It found no navigation errors, HTTP responses at or above 400, root overflow above 1 px, measured header/main/share bounds outside the viewport, or confirmed broken images. Its image counter included some zero-size hidden/deferred images at the viewport origin. The independent heading check preserved all 82 existing heading fragments across eight entries, with one H1 per entry and unique IDs.

Status terms: **verified** means the focused behavior passed in the latest recorded automated run; **covered** means a focused regression exists and passes, while a deployment or manual check remains.

## Deployed preview validation

The hosted validator passed **9/9 checks** on [the PR14 Workers preview](https://3cd1323b-romaingrx.romaingrx.workers.dev/). The seven representative public pages returned 200; three legacy routes returned 301 with canonical `Location` values; and an unknown route returned the branded 404. The hosted site served the expected content types for `favicon.ico`, `favicon.svg`, the Atkinson font, both sitemap files, `pagefind.js`, and `pagefind-entry.json`. RSS included blog and note items, and home/article Open Graph images were valid 1200×630 PNGs.

Hosted Pagefind returned the published article and note (two article results and one note result). Profile images were actual WebP variants at 128, 160, 256, and 320 px. The live Giscus widget changed from light to dark and back to light; its real `main[data-theme]`, theme stylesheet, and computed comment surfaces updated. The first-visit empty discussion UI rendered after a bounded wait; no comment or reaction was submitted.

The deployed responsive probe checked the 390 px Notes, Productivity notes tag, and ML blog category pages in light and dark themes. None had horizontal overflow. The Notes page contained three note links, the Productivity tag contained one, and the ML category showed ten taxonomy controls with `ML (4)`. Header controls and prose links changed with the settled theme; the dark category tag’s contrast and layout were visually reviewed. The Corne gallery opened with five photos, advanced to `Photo 2 of 5`, and loaded the second 1024×768 WebP; its dialog fit at 390 px, the 44×44 px close target remained visible, and there was no horizontal overflow. Checked photo responses were HTTP 200 `image/webp`. On the DDPM article, Continue Reading focused `article#article-intro`. The CUDA note showed its shared-tag Nix Flakes note; Corne’s no-match fallback showed the two other notes and excluded itself.

## Release gate

The local release checks are green: fresh pinned install, production build, route checks, lint, and the full three-engine suite (228/228), including committed-popup navigation readiness, focused reading scenarios, and controlled playback; the independent 82-fragment audit also passes. The hosted validator passed 9/9, and the deployed responsive and interaction checks above passed. The remaining release checks are actual 200% browser zoom and manual physical-device touch, soft-keyboard, screen-reader, and native-sharing behavior; these were not verified. A narrowed viewport is not treated as a substitute for browser zoom.
